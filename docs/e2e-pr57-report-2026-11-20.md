## Gaps

1. **Tao Nan grammar cloze boxes are numbered (1)–(4) while the passage says (21)–(24)** (`11-mock-taonan-cloze.png`). The set stores the key as the letter (`B`, `C`, …) from the option map, not the word (`down`, `in`, …). A student who types the word into box (1) is not answering blank (21), and the stored key is the letter. Flat cloze items (Catholic High 2024) do not have this problem: the box number matches the passage and the key is the word.
2. **Parent marks do not change the score.** The Papers tab still says `2/36 (auto-marked)` after Correct. That matches the current parent route, which only writes `parent_correct`. The Saturday credit was already decided at hand-in and did not change when the parent marked.
3. **Ai Tong passage-less set, question 3** ("Write 1, 2 and 3…") shows no boxes (`17-no-passage.png`). The other questions on that screen have a parent-marked box or True/False. The student cannot answer question 3.
4. **Past-paper mock header says "Nanyang Primary"** while the items are the Anglo-Chinese (Junior) paper (`02-practice-mcq.png`). The label comes from `MOCK_EXAMS[0]`, not from the plan.
5. **One practice editing hint is in Korean** ("persevere는 …") on an English screen (`07-practice-editing-marked.png`).
6. The effort check is still "half the time **or** 80% of the questions". A 2-second paper paid because 43/44 were answered. A short paper on the same Saturday did not pay. The old "only 14 items are graded, so clicking those pays" case did not recur: the unpaid paper's denominator was 36, and the paid paper's was 37.

## Small product fixes made while clicking

The mock paper rendered editing with choice buttons. After this PR those items have no options, so there was nowhere to type, and the misspelled word was not underlined. `PaperExam` now underlines the wrong word when it stands alone and shows a text box when there are fewer than two options. A punctuation key no longer gets a guessed underline (the closest-word search was highlighting "is").
